Kaelan Burkett

  • Women Dressing Women

    Fall 2023 • animation and media installation

    A “genealogy” of women designers and fashion houses, illustrating apprenticeships, associations, and shared legacies over time. Installed at The Metropolitan Museum of Art in the exhibition, Women Dressing Women.

  • 30th Avenue

    Fall 2023 – ongoing • website design and algorithmic video processing

    A living archive of video artifacts recorded along 30th Avenue in Queens. Each video is broadcast once daily, at the exact time of day when it was originally recorded.

  • Digital stereoscope

    Spring 2025 • animation and media installation

    A 19th-century wooden stereoscope viewer retrofitted with digital reproductions of contemporaneous photographs. Installed at the Metropolitan Museum of Art for the exhibition, The New Art: American Photography 1839–1910.

  • Counterpoise

    Winter 2026 • website design and programming

    A two-panel map showing disparate regions of earth at matching scale. Designed to offer a better understanding of spatial relativity and contrast with distortions commonly represented in global map projections.

  • Ledelle Moe

    Fall 2019 – Winter 2020 • photography and videography

    Photo and video documentation of the artist’s exhibition, When, at the Massachusetts Museum of Contemporary Art. Published as an exhibition catalogue.

  • Interference

    ? • photography

    A photo series illustrating interactions, interventions, and interfaces between human and non-human life forms on the visible landscape.

  • Recasting the Past

    Spring 2025 • animation and media installation

    A projected animation of ink rubbings taken from a 12th-century Song Dynasty bronze bell. The projection improves legibility of decorations and narrative scenes cast around the sides of the bell. Installed at the Metropolitan Museum of Art for the exhibition, Recasting the Past.
